July 2026 - Irrwicht IRW-1T
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Federated Suns Ministry of Information, Intelligence, and Operations (MIIO) found themselves operating under strong resource constraints following reclaiming of New Avalon and the Dragon's Tongue during Operation SOLAR FLARE. MIIO created task forces to investigate low cost approaches to deter aggression from the Federated Sun's historical enemies. The Covert Operations Division proposed designing an inexpensive BattleMech which could be supplied to rebels in neighboring realms to help destabilize their traditional rivals. The design team started with a 55 ton chassis, allowing the Irrwicht to share common components with many prolific designs, such as the Griffin, Shadow Hawk, and Wolverine BattleMechs. From there, the team focused on designing a platform for pilots with minimal military training but reasonable experience piloting agricultural or industrial 'Mechs. The resulting design proved highly popular in the Periphery, especially when paired with units with longer range capabilities, such as the Merlin. The initial production run used components from as many different manufacturers to demonstrate the ease of maintenance for this platform, with no two units in this production run containing identically matching components. The MIIO intended to use a shell company to collect licensing fees for the design to fund covert operations; however, the simplicity of the design proved easy to replicate and soon several Periphery manufacturers were creating unlicensed replicas.

In its first deployment, the base design, the Irrwicht IRW-1T, proved successful in border raids against the Raven Alliance from pirate forces operating out of the Outworlds Wastes. The Draconis Combine also encountered the design during in border raids in short order. Unfortunately, the rugged design rapidly proliferated, and the Federated Suns soon found themselves fighting their own design, much to the disappointment of MIIO. As these raiders captured 'Mechs equipped with clan technology, the various medium lasers on the Irrwicht often received field upgrades, resulting in a wide variance in range capabilities, complicating MIIO's efforts to provide intelligence on the unit to border militia units.

Juncus JS-93
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

The Outworlds Alliance historically has relied upon its superior aerospace pilots to protect their citizens from pirates and other external threats. However, the Alliance Military Corps remained concerned about the growing skill gap between their ground forces and those of neighboring realms or even some pirate bands. To this end, leadership commissioned the Juncus project - a fast, low observable VTOL platform designed to insert sharpshooters behind enemy lines to target commanders and specialists. Development of the Juncus proved to be fraught with problems, as the Outworlds Alliance does not have particularly many factories capable of producing military combat vehicles. Ultimately, the contract went to Kukk Transportation Industries, which was primarily known for their Sparrowhawk SPR-DH but proved to be up to the task of a limited production run of the Jancus.

The Juncus saw limited deployment with the Alliance Military Corps. However, the Outworlds Alliance Intelligence service found the platform well suited to their needs, especially with the Infiltration Division. The platform's low heat signature and mast mounted surveillance equipment made it ideal for stealthy insertions.

Given the principal usage of the Juncus is by the Outworlds Alliance Intelligence service, details about its deployment history are difficult to come by. While government officials reported the platform was successful at suppressing pirate raids, this has not been independently confirmed. The bulk of sightings of the Juncus in Outworlds Alliance territory have been on Dante and Baliggora, fueling suspicion that the platform has primarily seen usage against 'internal threats'.


Gallinago PA(L)
^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^^

Equipped with advanced camouflage and surveillance equipment, the Gallinago suits are designed for monitoring and eliminating high value targets. The limited jump-jet capabilities on the suits was added when the Juncus VTOL was added to the Outworlds Alliance Intelligence inventory.

When Outworlds Alliance Intelligence received two Tornado power armor suits, they were unsure if they should disassemble the suits for research or preserve them for special operations. Ultimately, OAI leadership decided to disassemble the suits to research and develop clones. While the the Gallinago suits have significantly reduced capabilities, they represent a significant advantage against their typical targets, ultimately proving the decision to disassemble the Tornado suits to be the right one for the OAI.

Unconfirmed sightings of the Gallinago have been reported on Dante and Baliggora.
